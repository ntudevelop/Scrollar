Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Scrollar ("we," "our," or "the App") is a screen time management application developed by NTU Develop. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with Apple's App Store Guidelines and applicable privacy laws.

2. Data We Collect

Scrollar is a local-first application. All personal data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Information you provide during setup:

  • First name, Age range, and Profession category
  • Screen time reduction goals and goal intensity
  • Avatar preference

Information generated through app use:

  • Daily screen time usage statistics (read via iOS Screen Time APIs)
  • App budget settings and group configurations
  • Usage history snapshots (automatically pruned after 30 days)
  • Pixel Pig progress (level, saved minutes, streaks)

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data listed above is stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database. We do not operate servers or cloud infrastructure that store your personal data. Your data is included in your standard iOS device backups (iCloud or local). Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

RevenueCat: Used to manage subscriptions. They receive transaction-related data (receipts, subscription status) via Apple but do not receive your screen time, usage stats, or profile info. View RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Apple App Store: Purchases are processed by Apple. View Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or behavioral tracking data.
  • We do not use crash reporting services or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not create user accounts or maintain server-side profiles.
  • We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties.
